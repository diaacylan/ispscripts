# Content quality checklist for ISP Scripts

This is a maintainer checklist for future releases. It is intentionally stricter than a page-count target.

## Before publishing a page

- The page answers one distinct question for a WISP or ISP operator.
- The author has added an original explanation, example, failure mode, or verification method.
- Commands are labeled with the RouterOS version or tested context.
- The page states what it cannot know about the reader's topology.
- The page links to the relevant generator or guide without replacing the explanation with a call to action.
- The page has a clear title, description, canonical URL, author, review date, and one useful H1.
- The page is linked from a relevant hub and included in the sitemap.

## Before requesting an AdSense review

- Every navigation item works on desktop and mobile.
- No article is a stub, a duplicate, or a lightly rewritten copy of another page.
- Tools explain their inputs, output, limitations, and safe test procedure.
- About, Contact, Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer, and Changelog pages are reachable from the footer.
- Search Console can crawl the canonical URLs and the sitemap is current.
- Ads, if enabled, do not interrupt configuration fields or safety-critical controls.
