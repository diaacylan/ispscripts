What's changed

A running record of what's been added to ispscripts.com, in order. No silent changes — if a generator's output logic changes, it's listed here.

  • Latest update
    Homepage messaging refresh
    Rewrote the headline on all three generator pages to lead with what you get, added "Free · Runs in your browser · Tested on RouterOS v7.x" trust badges, and linked the public source repo from every page.
  • Recent
    Compliance pages expanded
    About page now covers why the site exists and who it's for. Contact page clarifies why there's no contact form. Privacy policy states plainly that there's no backend server for the generators to send data to.
  • Recent
    Blog section launched
    Added a blog index and the first article, "Securing MikroTik Routers Against Common Attacks." Also added beginner-friendly explanations under each config block on all three generator pages.
  • PPPoE vs Hotspot comparison added
    A side-by-side article to help operators pick the right subscriber access model.
  • NAT + Firewall Generator added
    A third generator for any MikroTik gateway — masquerade, optional port forwarding, WAN-side service hardening, and brute-force protection.
  • Trust pages added
    About, Privacy Policy, and Contact pages published.
  • Hotspot + RADIUS Generator added
    Second generator, for voucher/card-based captive portal access, with its own setup guide.
  • Site launched
    First generator published: PPPoE + RADIUS config generator, with an in-depth setup guide.

Every generator's output is reviewed against a real RouterOS v7.x device before publishing. If a script ever produces invalid syntax on your version, let me know — that's exactly what this changelog and the contact page are for.

Written for WISP and ISP operators running MikroTik + RADIUS. Not affiliated with MikroTik or any RADIUS platform vendor.
