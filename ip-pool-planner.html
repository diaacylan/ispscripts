Convert an address block into a pool plan that leaves room for growth and operations.

Free to use · Runs locally in your browser · Last reviewed 2026-09-26

How to use this result
The planner distinguishes total usable addresses from the number you intend to allocate. For CGNAT subscriber addressing, 100.64.0.0/10 is shared address space; for ordinary private LANs use RFC1918 ranges and document the routing boundary.
Do not place two overlapping pools in adjacent routers unless the traffic is intentionally isolated. Check the final range against customer VLANs, VPNs, and upstream routes.
