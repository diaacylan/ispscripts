Simple Queue Generator for MikroTik

Create one understandable subscriber queue and learn exactly what each RouterOS limit controls.

Free to use · Runs locally in your browser · Last reviewed 2026-09-26
Review before deployment: Simple Queues are easy to audit, but they can conflict with existing queues, FastTrack, or another shaping layer. Generate one narrow rule and verify its counters.

Inputs

simple-queue.rsc
Change an input to recalculate.
How to use this result
RouterOS reads max-limit from the router perspective: upload/download in this form become up/down. Use a known test subscriber and watch /queue simple print stats. If counters stay at zero, check target, FastTrack, and whether another queue owns the traffic.
